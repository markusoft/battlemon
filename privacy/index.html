Battlemon Privacy Policy

Effective November 23, 2026

Publisher: ease-z
Contact: mark.prosoftphils@gmail.com

Scope

This policy applies to Battlemon, including its Free features and optional one-time Full unlock. Free includes offline Monster Showdown, One of each type or Neutral only selection, and Iconic artwork. Full adds other game modes, monster selection options, local multiplayer, replays, and Original artwork.

Offline play and information on your device

Battlemon stores your chosen trainer name, preferences, saved game, and supported replays in app storage. These are used to run and resume your games. No publisher account is required. Offline gameplay does not send this information to an ease-z server. We do not operate a public online game server or automatic cloud synchronization service for this app.

Local multiplayer in Full

When you choose to host or join a LAN game, participating devices exchange your chosen trainer name, room details, seat information, game actions, and game state. Local IP addresses are visible to devices communicating on that network. Hosts and players may retain names and actions in their saved games or replays. These user-directed transfers are needed to play together; ease-z does not receive or centrally store the session. LAN currently uses unencrypted HTTP and WebSocket connections. Use trusted Wi-Fi or a private hotspot, and avoid using sensitive personal information as a trainer or room name.

Hosting notification and background operation

Starting a LAN host starts an Android foreground service. An ongoing notification lets you return to the hosted game or stop hosting. The service keeps the game and local connection running while the app is in the background and uses wake and Wi-Fi locks while hosting. Hosting ends when you choose Stop hosting or Android stops the app or service. Notification permission controls whether Android can display the notification; you can change it in Android Settings.

Camera and selected files

If you choose Scan when joining a game, the camera is used to decode the game invitation on your device. This feature does not save or upload camera images. Camera permission is optional and can be denied or revoked. Single-player gameplay does not need camera access. Replay export and other supported file operations use a system picker and files you explicitly choose or save. Exported files stay where you save them; sharing through another app is your choice and is also governed by that app's policies.

Optional Full purchase

Google Play handles the one-time Full purchase and payment-account information under its own policies. Battlemon queries the product price and purchase status, verifies the signed purchase record, and acknowledges purchases through Google Play Billing. A verified purchase record is stored privately on your device to keep confirmed Full access available offline. Restore Purchase checks ownership using the purchasing Google Play account. Purchase information is not sent to ease-z, and ease-z does not receive payment-card details. Refunded or revoked purchases may lose Full access when ownership is refreshed. Internet access and Google Play are needed to purchase or restore. Restoring Full does not restore deleted local game data or transfer saves between devices.

Advertising and analytics

Battlemon has no advertising, analytics SDK, publisher login, or publisher-operated gameplay telemetry service. We do not sell personal information or use advertising identifiers. The app does not request location, contacts, or microphone access. Google Play and Android may process installation, purchase, update, backup, and diagnostic information under your device settings and their own policies.

Retention and deletion

Local data remains until it is replaced, deleted with an available in-app control, cleared through Android app-storage settings, or removed by uninstalling the app. Clearing app storage removes local games, settings, replays, and cached purchase records. It does not cancel a Google Play purchase. Exported or shared files must be deleted separately. Other players may retain their own game or replay copies; ask them to delete those copies. ease-z cannot remotely delete files stored only on your device or another player's device. If you email support, we use your correspondence to answer your request and retain it as needed for support and applicable obligations. You can request deletion of support correspondence using the contact address.

Children

The app does not create accounts or knowingly request children's personal details. Trainer names can be nicknames; avoid entering personal information. A parent or guardian can contact ease-z about privacy questions.

This website and platform services

The published website and privacy policy are hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process website access information under its privacy statement. The site does not add advertising, analytics, cookies, or third-party embedded media; the LAN demonstration video is hosted with the site. The app does not automatically open the website or upload game information to it. Google Play, Android, GitHub, and optional email or file-sharing apps operate under their own policies.

Changes and contact

We will update this policy and its effective date when app behavior changes. The current policy is available from the app's home screen and the Battlemon website. For privacy questions or support, email mark.prosoftphils@gmail.com.
