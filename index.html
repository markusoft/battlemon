BRING YOUR ARENA ALONG · FULL VERSION

Same Wi-Fi.
Friendly rivals.
Real stakes.

Host on an Android phone, share the room’s QR code and let friends take their seats.

  1. Connect your devices to the same Wi-Fi or phone hotspot.
  2. Choose Host Local Game, set the seats and start hosting.
  3. Friends choose Join Local Game and scan the host’s invitation.

The hosting notification lets you return to the table or stop hosting. Hosting continues while the app is in the background.

Watch the LAN hosting demonstration

LAN is available with Full. Use a trusted local network that allows device-to-device connections. LAN traffic is not encrypted.
